Artists Brands

Neen

An artist that blends indie pop with human complexity and the organic world. The project started from her songs and the natural metaphors that run through them, then moved into an exploration of the living world as a way of naming emotions that words alone cannot reach.

From there, the ideation gathered into a mood board of organic textures, atmospheric references and a tender, contemplative tonality, brought together in a visual universe that echoes the music. The direction now supports neen's identity across the project's existing imagery and applications, and all of her music is gathered at neen.fm, a single link that redirects to every streaming platform.

Eliott Ray

An artist that makes intimate, electric and unapologetically sensitive alternative pop. The ideation started from the tension at the centre of his music: intimate emotions carried by frontal, electric energy.

The exploration looked for a way to articulate that contradiction without smoothing it out, moving through graphic experimentation, expressive imagery and live performance. That path produced a distinctive visual territory that keeps the rawness of the songs while making the artist's singularity more legible. The direction now serves the energy, the vulnerability and the unconventional spirit that define the project.

Suze

An artist that wraps Blondie weirdness and a cosmovintage soul in ultra-pop. Because the project is still taking shape, the ideation was built as an open system rather than a fixed identity: pop immediacy next to otherworldly references, familiar imagery next to eccentric details, playful energy next to cosmic nostalgia.

The work mapped these recurring contrasts in a mood board, tested visual directions and kept the strongest ones as possibilities rather than rules. What the direction establishes today is a coherent creative frame, one that can absorb new images, collaborations and formats as SuZe's universe evolves.

Baala

A fashion brand rooted in Tunisian heritage and the urban life of Paris and London. The ideation began with the question of how to hold two cultural realities together without turning heritage into a decorative motif.

The exploration gathered Tunisian references, materials and attitudes in a mood board, then translated them into a contemporary visual language that balances rootedness with movement and craftsmanship with the pace of city life. The resulting direction expresses that dual belonging with confidence, through the brand's imagery and identity work. The collaboration also covered the brand's photoshoots, the design of its newsletter and the organisation of its Fashion Week event, and Baala's universe can be discovered at studiobaala.com.

Pollen

A café in London that blends Danish minimalism with local organic materials, wood and traditional embroidered tablecloths. The ideation started from a single contrast: restraint and warmth.

From there, a mood board set the clarity of Danish design against the character of local, organic materials, using wood and embroidered tablecloths as tangible, familiar counterpoints to an understated language. The direction translates that balance into a coherent identity, one where every application reinforces the same idea: a contemporary café experience grounded in material warmth, simplicity and a sense of place.
